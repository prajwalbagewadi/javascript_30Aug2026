1. Main use: Backend, enterprise, Android
2. Learing curve: Medium-Hard
3. Backend: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
4. Frontend: ❌
5. APIs: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
6. Enterprise jobs: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
7. Protyping: ⭐⭐⭐
